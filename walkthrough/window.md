# Window, slices and views

- Drag on the image: left and right change the width, up and down change the level.
- Keys 1 to 4 pick CT brain (W 80, L 40), soft tissue (W 400, L 40), lung (W 1500, L -600) and bone (W 1800, L 400). Key 0 picks auto.
- Scroll, the arrow keys or the slider move through slices. Ctrl or Cmd and scroll zooms. Shift and drag pans.
- A, C and S switch to axial, coronal and sagittal. Reformatted views use the slice spacing, so proportions stay right.
- The bar under the image shows the voxel under the cursor, in HU for CT, with the stored value next to it.
